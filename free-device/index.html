How to test Gold AI on a free device

1

Open TestKase

Tap the TestKase card above and sign in — no card needed.

2

Upload the Gold AI test APK

Use the V21 test APK already sent to you (app-direct-debug.apk, 50 MB). Pick Galaxy S24 or Pixel 8 Pro.

3

Turn Always Listening ON

Open Gold AI → Settings → Always Listening ON, then wait 60 seconds.

4

Report crash or no crash

If the app closes, take a screenshot. If it keeps running for 60 seconds, the wake-word crash fix works.

One limit to know: no cloud lab can test the real voice wake-word — there is no microphone audio in the cloud. These labs can only prove whether Always Listening ON crashes the app. The Hindi voice and accent still need a test on your own phone.

Sessions are time-limited — watch the timer on screen, and close extra reserved devices so your free minutes are not wasted.
